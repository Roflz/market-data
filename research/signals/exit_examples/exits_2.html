How to read a chart
  • Yellow box = the entry pattern; yellow triangle = entry fill at the next bar's open.
  • Magenta box = the opposite signal that closed the trade (named on the chart and in the panel); magenta X = exit fill at the next bar's open. Dotted line between them = the trade, green if it made money after costs.
  • Blue-grey shading = time in the trade. EMA 8 red dashed, EMA 20 orange dashed, EMA 50 green dashed, VWAP cyan, Momentum(14) underneath. Dashed vertical lines = new session (18:00 ET).
  • No stop is used here, so each trade shows the pure signal exit. $ per 1 micro contract.
