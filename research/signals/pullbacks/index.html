Pullbacks in a trend: summary over all detected pullbacks (5-bar swings for the leg)

Each market / timeframe below counts every pullback found in the whole data set. Continuation % = share that went past SH (the leg's extreme) before closing past PL (the pullback low/high); Failure % = closed past PL first; Neither = neither within the window. Avg best / worst move = largest move for / against the trade from the event close within the window (up to 40 bars, same session, flat 16:45 ET), measured over the whole window in any order; Avg $ to SH / to PL = how far SH and PL were from the event close (what a target at SH would make and a stop at PL would risk, before costs). All $ are per 1 micro contract.

Pullback definition (bullish; bearish is the mirror)

  1. Impulse leg: a 5-bar swing low SL, then a 5-bar swing high SH, same contract; SL is the lowest and SH the highest price between them; EMA 8 > EMA 20 > EMA 50 at the SH bar.
  2. Pullback low PL: the first 3-bar swing low after SH. It must stay above SL, be the lowest price since SH, and no bar may close above SH before the setup is known.
  3. Depth = (SH - PL) / (SH - SL), kept between 0.1 and 1.
  4. Event = PL + 3 bars (the swing low is confirmed); if SH itself is confirmed later, the event waits for that.
  5. What happened next: from the event close, up to 40 bars in the same session, flat by 16:45 ET.

How to read a chart

  • White arrow = the impulse leg, from the leg start to the leg high (low for bearish). Yellow ring = the pullback low (high for bearish); its yellow dashed line is the level that, if price CLOSES past it, counts as a failure. The solid grey line at the leg high is the level that counts as a continuation once price trades past it.
  • Dashed grey lines = Fibonacci retracements of the leg (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%), labelled on the right.
  • White vertical line = the moment the setup is known (3 bars after the pullback low). Everything in the blue-grey area to its right is what happened next; the green dot / red X marks the bar where it continued or failed.
  • EMA 8 red dashed, EMA 20 orange dashed, EMA 50 green dashed, VWAP cyan. Thin dashed vertical lines = a new session (18:00 ET).
  • Bottom panel: Momentum(14) = 100 × close / close 14 bars earlier; dots at the leg start, leg end and pullback. A yellow dashed line = hidden divergence (higher low in price, lower momentum).
  • The panel on the right lists every stat; $ are per 1 micro contract (MES $5, MNQ $2, MYM $0.50 per point), before costs.
