How to read a chart

How to read a chart

  • White arrow = the impulse leg, from the leg start to the leg high (low for bearish). Yellow ring = the pullback low (high for bearish); its yellow dashed line is the level that, if price CLOSES past it, counts as a failure. The solid grey line at the leg high is the level that counts as a continuation once price trades past it.
  • Dashed grey lines = Fibonacci retracements of the leg (38.2%, 50%, 61.8%), labelled on the right.
  • White vertical line = the moment the setup is known (3 bars after the pullback low). Everything in the blue-grey area to its right is what happened next; the green dot / red X marks the bar where it continued or failed.
  • EMA 8 red dashed, EMA 20 orange dashed, EMA 50 green dashed, VWAP cyan. Thin dashed vertical lines = a new session (18:00 ET).
  • Bottom panel: Momentum(14) = 100 × close / close 14 bars earlier; dots at the leg start, leg end and pullback. A yellow dashed line = hidden divergence (higher low in price, lower momentum).
  • The panel on the right lists every stat; $ are per 1 micro contract (MES $5, MNQ $2, MYM $0.50 per point), before costs.
