# Every Trading Strategy Explained in 12 Minutes: key content and testable rules

Not included (no tradeable content, or weak evidence): intro/transition screens, moon phases, Renko chart settings, Gann angles, the outro.

## Fibonacci retracements (slides 2, 3)
Rule: after a swing low → swing high (confirmed swings), wait for a pullback to a retracement level; the video says 0.382 is 'the most common level where price tends to reverse', and to combine it with other confirmation. Testable: bounce rate at 0.382 / 0.5 / 0.618 levels vs random levels (like the swing study). Fits your pullback idea directly.

## Breakout patterns (slides 5, 6, 7, 8)
Consolidation, then a sharp move out of it. Patterns: rectangle, triangle, flag, wedge. Testable: detect a range (N bars inside X ATR, or converging swing highs/lows) and measure what follows a close outside it. Note: our data showed small inside-bar/NR7 breakouts tend to FAIL on 5-min.

## Reversal patterns (slides 10, 11, 12, 13)
Double/triple tops and bottoms, head & shoulders, cup & handle. Testable from swing points: two or three swing highs/lows within a tolerance, then a break of the neckline (the swing between them).

## Elliott wave (slides 16, 17, 18, 19)
Rules shown: wave 2 cannot retrace more than wave 1 (usually ~0.618), wave 3 is the longest of 1/3/5, wave 4 stays above the wave-1 peak (usually ~0.382). Entry idea: buy the wave-4 pullback at 0.382. Codeable from swing sequences, but subjective; lower priority.

## Fair value gaps (slides 21, 22)
A big-bodied candle leaves a gap between the wick of the candle before and the wick of the candle after; that zone 'acts as a magnet' where price may revisit before continuing. Testable: 3-candle gap (bar1 high < bar3 low for bullish); revisit rate and what happens at the revisit.

## Candlestick patterns (slides 24, 25, 26, 27)
Engulfing = strong momentum in its direction; hammer / shooting star = rejection (long wick); doji = neutral. Already detected; our data says on their own they are near coin flips.

## Heikin Ashi (slides 29, 30)
Smoothed candles: green = uptrend, red = downtrend, bigger body = stronger trend; less noise but not the real price. Testable as a trend or exit filter (exit when the Heikin Ashi colour flips).

## Harmonic patterns (slides 46, 48, 49)
XABCD shapes with Fibonacci ratio rules (bullish bat: XB 0.382-0.5 of XA, AC 0.382-0.886, ...); buy at D. Codeable from swing points; many free ratios, so high risk of finding patterns by chance.

## Support & resistance (slides 51, 52)
Horizontal levels where price bounced before: buy near support, sell near resistance. This is what the swing-size study measures (bounce vs break at swing levels).

## Dynamic support & resistance (slides 54)
Moving averages (or VWAP) acting as support in a trend. Your EMA pullback idea; testable as bounce rate at EMA 20/50 and VWAP touches.

## Trend lines (slides 56, 57, 58)
Line through swing lows (uptrend) or highs (downtrend); a retrace to the line is a buy (or sell) opportunity. Codeable: line through the last two confirmed swing lows, touch and bounce/break stats.

## Momentum indicators (trend) (slides 68, 71, 72, 73, 74)
MACD crossover, price above/below a moving average, Parabolic SAR dots, Supertrend colour. Video: most effective in TRENDING markets. Easy to compute and test as trend filters or exit signals.

## Oscillators (range) (slides 77, 79, 80)
RSI and Stochastic overbought/oversold = possible reversal. Video: most effective in CHOPPY/SIDEWAYS markets. Idea worth testing: use oscillators only when the market is ranging and momentum indicators only when it is trending.

## Divergence (slides 83, 84, 85, 87)
Price makes a higher high but the indicator a lower high (bearish), or the mirror (bullish) = possible reversal. Codeable with confirmed swing points + momentum/RSI/MACD values at those swings. Strong candidate for your 'is it a pullback or a reversal' question and for exits.

## Volume (slides 91, 92, 94)
Volume shows the strength behind a move; VWAP as dynamic support/resistance; volume profile (volume by price) shows key levels. Codeable from 1-minute volume: volume spikes on rejection candles, VWAP touches, high-volume price nodes as levels.

## Supply & demand (order blocks) (slides 96, 97)
Zone where price left sharply upward = demand, sharply downward = supply; treat as key levels. Codeable: the base candles just before a strong impulse; test revisits.

## Market structure, BOS, CHoCH (slides 98, 99, 100)
Uptrend = higher highs and higher lows. Break of structure = breaking the previous high in the trend (continuation). Change of character = breaking the previous structure the other way, e.g. a lower low in an uptrend (possible reversal). Codeable straight from confirmed swing points; a natural trend definition, entry trigger and exit signal for your style.
