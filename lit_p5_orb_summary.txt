P5  OPENING-RANGE BREAKOUT USING THE FIRST 1-5 MINUTES   (literature_report.md section 7 'P5.'; preregistration.md Step 5)
======================================================================================================================
Data: Databento CME 1-min bars, continuous front by volume, unadjusted; ES -> MES ($5/pt), NQ -> MNQ ($2/pt).
Costs: market/stop fills 1 tick adverse; limit target needs a 1-tick trade-through, no slippage; $1.00 per round trip. Per 1 micro.
Gross = no slippage, no commission (entry at bar open / stop-order price, exit at stop/target/close price).
bp per trade = points / entry reference price x 1e4. R-multiples use R = |entry fill - stop|.
Day-level t = mean daily net $ / Newey-West(5 lags, Bartlett) SE, over ALL trading days of the period (no-trade days = 0).
Exchange holidays (RTH bars end <= 13:05: Globex-only session, no cash open) are not trading days. Early-close days (RTH bars end 13:14/13:15)
  are traded with the report's early-close rule from P1 (flat at the 12:59 bar close; P5 itself names none, and all P5 entries are before 11:00).
Abnormal days (>= 5 consecutive missing RTH minutes) are excluded and logged (report section 5.6).
Random-direction control: on every strategy trade, the same entry bar and reference price, a coin-flip direction (1 tick adverse), the same R,
  stop R away, 10R target (if the variant has one) and the same close exit. For family B the opposite side enters at market at the breakout price.
  'Average of both directions' = mean of the always-long and always-short versions of that bracket.
Family B cost filter uses the planned R (range + 2 ticks), known before the OCO orders are placed; family A uses R from the actual entry fill.
